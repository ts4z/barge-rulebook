# Sviten Special

*This section is new and may have errors.*

Also known as Celestial Poker, or Céleste in French.

## TLDR

[Dramaha](dramaha.md), except that a player who draws exactly one card sees
the replacement face up and may reject it for another.

## Forced Money

The game is played with a button and blinds.

## Dealing Procedure

Each player is dealt five cards face down, followed by a round of betting at
the small bet limit.

Three community cards are placed face up. This is followed by a round of
betting at the small bet limit.

Then each player in turn discards zero to five cards from their hand and is
dealt an equal number of replacement cards face down (unless the discard
is exactly one card).

A player who discards exactly one card is dealt the replacement face up.
Before the next player draws, that player must either keep the face-up card or
reject it. A rejected card is discarded and the player is dealt another
replacement face down, which must be kept.

One additional community card is placed face up. This is followed by a round of
betting at the big bet limit.

One additional community card is placed face up. This is followed by a round of
betting at the big bet limit. This is followed by a showdown.

## Evaluating the Showdown

The best [Omaha](omaha-high-only.md) high hand (using exactly two cards from
the player's hand and exactly three cards from the board) and the best
[Five-card Draw](five-card-draw.md) hand (using all five cards from the
player’s hand) split the pot.

## Variations

Sviten Flush is Sviten Special where the draw half of the pot goes to the best
flush instead of the best five-card poker hand. A player's flush is made from
the cards of a single suit in their hand; their other cards are ignored.

A flush with more cards beats a flush with fewer cards. Flushes with the same
number of cards are compared by their highest card, then their next highest
card, and so on, with aces high. Straights don't count, so A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♠ T♠ is
the best possible flush and 6♥ 5♥ 4♥ 3♥ 2♥ is merely the worst five-card
flush.

Example: Player A holds K♥ 9♥ 6♥ 2♥ Q♠ and Player B holds A♣ J♣ 8♣ 4♣ 3♦.
Both have four-card flushes, and Player B's ace-high flush is better. Player C
holds 7♠ 5♠ 4♠ 3♠ 2♠, a five-card flush, and beats both.

Since there are only four suits, every five-card hand contains at least a
two-card flush.

## Notes

Sviten Special is usually played [pot-limit](pot-limit.md).

Sviten Special is widely credited to Anders "Bengan" Bengtsson, who devised it
in 2004 at his Sviten poker club in Stockholm. It is popular in the Nordic
countries, but an essentially identical game was played in French Canada in
the 1980s. See [Pagat](https://www.pagat.com/poker/variants/sviten.html) and
[PokerNews](https://www.pokernews.com/strategy/how-to-play-sviten-special-poker-rules-gameplay-strategy-52241.htm).

Sviten Flush comes from [a
post](https://billspoker.substack.com/p/sviten-flush-new-variant-of-a-new) on
Bill Mawer's [All the Chips](https://billspoker.substack.com/) Substack.
