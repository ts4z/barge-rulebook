# Bridgewater

*This section is new and may have errors.*

## TLDR

[Omaha](omaha-high-only.md) in which players may use anywhere from zero to
three of their hole cards.  Dealt with either four or five hole cards.

## Forced Money

The game is played with a button and blinds.

## Dealing Procedure

Each player is dealt either four or five cards face down, depending on which
version is being played, followed by a round of betting at the small bet limit.

Three community cards are placed face up. This is followed by a round of
betting at the small bet limit.

One additional community card is placed face up. This is followed by a round of
betting at the big bet limit.

One additional community card is placed face up. This is followed by a round of
betting at the big bet limit. This is followed by a showdown.

## Evaluating the Showdown

The best high five-card hand wins the pot. Hands are made using zero, one, two,
or three cards from each player’s hand and the remaining cards from the board.

## Notes

Compare [Irish](irish.md), in which players may use zero, one, or two hole
cards, and [Omaha](omaha-high-only.md), in which players must use exactly two.
